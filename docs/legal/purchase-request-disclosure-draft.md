# Equipment purchase process disclosure

Draft version: purchase-request-2026-10-11. Prepared for review; not a representation of counsel approval. Activate only with the linked purchase agreement, payment, privacy and electronic-record policies reviewed together.

## Confirm Your Purchase Request

You are requesting to begin the purchase process for the equipment identified in your checkout through Vendibook.

1. You are voluntarily submitting a purchase request to the identified seller.
2. The seller must review and approve the request. A change to the price or included items requires your separate acceptance; the seller cannot silently substitute different terms.
3. Following approval, an invoice issued on behalf of the seller will identify the equipment price, applicable tax, agreed delivery or freight charges, and any other disclosed costs.
4. No payment is collected when you submit this request. Review the invoice and separately authorize payment. Seller approval alone is not a payment instruction.
5. A request does not guarantee availability, reserve equipment or complete a sale. An approved invoice identifies any reservation and its payment deadline. An expired or canceled unpaid invoice may release that reservation.
6. The identified equipment seller remains the legal seller. Vendibook provides marketplace coordination, records and the payment interface. Responsibilities concerning condition, title, included items, fulfillment, cancellation and remedies are governed by the transaction documents you separately accept and applicable law.

Required unchecked acknowledgment:

“I understand that I am expressly requesting to initiate a purchase transaction through Vendibook. I understand that seller approval and a separate invoice payment are required before the purchase can proceed.”

## Invoice and handoff disclosure

The invoice is payable only through its authenticated Vendibook payment page. Review the seller identity, equipment, agreed terms, destination, itemized charges and payment deadline before paying. A request or invoice is not a receipt; a receipt is issued after confirmed payment.

For eligible connected-merchant transactions using delayed disbursement, PayPal processes the payment and controls the delayed release under its supported capabilities and policies. Vendibook does not provide an escrow account or hold funds indefinitely. PayPal documents automatic disbursement after 28 days. Delivery delays or unresolved issues approaching that deadline require prompt review; opening a Vendibook support case does not itself guarantee that PayPal will extend the holding period. Your rights under applicable law and PayPal's applicable policies are not waived by this disclosure.

Confirm successful delivery or handoff only after inspecting the equipment and completing the required transaction steps. If there is a problem, use Report an Issue instead. Do not confirm a successful transfer merely to request support. Fulfillment confirmations and signatures are recorded with the transaction.

## Separate electronic records consent

Reuse the current Checkout Privacy & Electronic Consent process. Do not combine this purchase-request acknowledgment with an automatic acceptance of all electronic-record terms. Counsel should confirm the disclosure of scope, withdrawal, paper copies, contact updates, hardware/software requirements and the method of demonstrating access where consumer-consent rules apply.

## Review checklist

- Verify legal seller identity and the contract formation point against the purchase agreement and local requirements.
- Confirm cancellation, reservation, refund and dispute provisions match implemented behavior.
- Confirm fee disclosures, tax responsibility and any transaction exclusions.
- Retain the exact accepted version, parties, listing, request, transaction terms and acceptance timestamp with access controls and the approved retention policy.

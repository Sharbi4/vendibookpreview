# Buyer Personas: Vendibook Buyer Desk

Evidence comes from 30d clean traffic (753 sessions, 44–87% mobile on top listings), search events and asset requests. Each persona lists the trigger to watch for, the objections that stop contact, and the messaging that answers them.

## P1: First-time operator (financing-led)
- **Who:** Starting a first concept (coffee, lemonade, tacos, shaved ice). Works a day job and browses on a phone at night. Comes from FB groups and Google.
- **Budget:** $5k–$25k. This is the most crowded demand band: trailers under $20k get 7–17 sessions per listing.
- **Says:** "ISO starter trailer", "how much to start a food truck", "can I finance", "do I need a commissary".
- **Blockers:** fear of buying junk or a scam (blank title or condition), not knowing what passes health inspection, the login wall before asking a "dumb question", and the large one-time cost.
- **Messaging that works:** "Clean title · Ready to inspect · Ask the seller anything, no account needed." Show price as both total and estimated monthly cost. Lead with "start under $X". Name the use case ("fairs and farmers' markets").
- **Listings to show:** Lemonade 6x12 NC $5.9k, Aiken SC $8k, Placerville coffee $14k, Frostproof FL $18k.

## P2: Expanding operator
- **Who:** Already runs a truck, trailer or restaurant and wants unit #2 or a bigger kitchen. Knows equipment and moves fast when the specs are right.
- **Budget:** $30k–$120k.
- **Says:** "second truck", "upgrade to a bigger trailer", "need a hood + fire suppression".
- **Blockers:** missing specs (year, make, mileage, hood/suppression, generator wattage, insignia/permits), and offers that are turned off or expire silently.
- **Messaging that works:** spec-forward copy with no fluff. "Runs & drives · Fire suppression tagged · Make an offer." Answer within 24h.
- **Listings to show:** Hiram GA truck $30k, SF coffee $43k, SDG 37' MI $118.5k, Freightliner VA $35.5k.

## P3: Event / catering renter
- **Who:** Caterers, event planners and new operators testing a concept. Needs a unit for a weekend, a season, or 1–3 months.
- **Budget:** $125–$400/day; a monthly rate is a strong pull. The Lilburn GA listing with a $5k/mo rate got the contact; Rex GA without one did not.
- **Says:** "rent a food trailer for my event", "lease a food truck for the summer", "rent-to-own".
- **Blockers:** no monthly price, few photos, disclosed broken equipment at full price, no clear delivery or pickup terms. Biggest gap: Twin Cities, MN (17 zero-result sessions), plus Houston and Atlanta.
- **Messaging that works:** "Available [dates] · $X/day, $Y/month · Pickup or delivery within Z miles." Lead with what they can serve on day one.
- **Listings to show:** Lilburn GA, Rex GA, Houston taco trailer, Tomball bar trailer, Austin BBQ truck.

## P4: Commissary / ghost kitchen seeker
- **Who:** Food truck operators who need a required commissary base, delivery-only brands, and caterers. B2B, recurring monthly spend.
- **Budget:** $100–$400/day, $750–$3k per month.
- **Says:** "commissary kitchen near me", "shared kitchen rental", "ghost kitchen LA/Houston".
- **Blockers:** no supply in their metro (zero results in LA, Houston and Colorado Springs). Also unclear hours, storage and parking for trucks.
- **Messaging that works:** "Licensed commercial kitchen · Truck parking · Cold/dry storage · Hourly or monthly." Pair it with truck and trailer listings ("Buying a truck? You'll need a commissary; here's one nearby").
- **Listings to show:** Phoenix ($100/day), Las Vegas ($100/day), Charlotte, Dallas, Boaz AL.

## Cross-persona rules
1. Never ask for an account before the first question (all personas, worst for P1 on mobile).
2. Show the same 4 trust facts everywhere: title status, condition, running/operational status, permit/insignia.
3. Social copy follows the persona: P1 gets the dream plus a low starting price, P2 gets specs, P3 gets dates and rates, P4 gets compliance and logistics.

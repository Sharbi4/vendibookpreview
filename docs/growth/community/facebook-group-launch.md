## Rules

1. **Mobile food industry only.** Posts must relate to food trucks, food trailers, commercial or commissary kitchens, catering or mobile food equipment, or closely related services.
2. **Include location.** Sale, rental and wanted posts must include city and state.
3. **Include price when possible.** Give the sale price, rental price or budget, or say "price on request".
4. **No misleading listings.** Represent equipment, availability, condition, ownership and pricing accurately.
5. **No spam.** Don't repost the same listing repeatedly or post unrelated promotions.
6. **Be professional.** No harassment, discrimination, scams or abuse.
7. **Rental posts state terms.** Rental period, pricing, deposit, and pickup or delivery, when known.
8. **Kitchen posts state availability.** City, available days and hours, main equipment, and any insurance or business requirements.
9. **Transact safely.** Verify the equipment, identity, ownership, title, agreement, insurance and licensing before paying. Never send a deposit to someone you haven't verified, and never pay by gift card, crypto or wire to a stranger.
10. **Vendibook listings are welcome, never required.** Post directly here. Vendibook also offers listing, booking, verification, financing and rental tools if they help.

## Moderation

Admin assist: decline posts containing the keywords below and send them to review. Review new members' first post. Mute obvious spam accounts.

**Review keywords:** gift card, crypto, bitcoin, usdt, wire transfer, western union, zelle only, cash app only, shipping agent, escrow agent, overpay, "send deposit to hold", "DM for price only", whatsapp me.

**Review if:** no city or state on a sale or rental post; the same photos as another member's post; a brand-new profile with a sale over $20k; the same text in several posts.

**Never remove** legitimate posts from competitors, dealers or sellers because they don't use Vendibook. Trust is the product.

## Growing the group without spam

- Invite existing Vendibook sellers and renters first (on-platform message, not a mass email).
- Join food truck, trailer, catering, chef and shared-kitchen groups as Vendibook staff. Answer questions helpfully and mention the group only when someone asks where to find rentals, buyers or kitchens. Never drop links in other groups' feeds.
- Ask builders and dealers to share it with customers (they get a DEALER / BUILDER topic).
- Culinary schools and community-college programs: offer the group as a resource for students.

## How Vendibook shows up

Help first. Mention Vendibook only when it solves the problem:
- A trailer hasn't sold: "Have you thought about renting it while you wait? Vendibook supports rental listings too."
- Someone asks where to rent: give real options first, Vendibook among them.
- A restaurant mentions empty hours: Vendibook is building shared-kitchen inventory. Would they list the hours?

Never send members their own listing view counts, and never share members' contact details outside the group.

## What to log (weekly, into the growth sheet)

Log one row per signal: date, post link, city/state, role (buyer, renter, seller, owner open to renting, kitchen host, chef), equipment (truck, trailer or kitchen), concept, rental length, budget, kitchen hours available, and objections (insurance, deposit, damage, trust). No phone numbers or emails in the sheet. Contact happens through Facebook or Vendibook messages.

Top requested cities and equipment go into the weekly liquidity review. Each renter lead goes to the Buyer Desk, and each owner open to renting or kitchen host goes to the Supply Desk.
